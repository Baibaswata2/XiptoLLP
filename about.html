About us

Technology built close to the people who use it.

Xipto LLP is a technology company based in Kailashahar, Unakoti, Tripura. We create digital products that make everyday life easier, safer and more connected for local communities.

What we do

Practical products for real-world needs

Build

We design and develop software and digital services, from the first idea through to a product people can use every day.

Serve communities

We start from local problems in Tripura and build solutions that fit the way people actually live and travel.

Earn trust

Safety, reliability and clear communication guide how we build and how we support our users.

Our approach

Local solutions for brighter communities

We believe good technology should solve a problem someone actually has. That is why we focus on products with a strong local impact, starting with LiphtUP, our ride booking platform for Tripura.

As we grow, we will keep the same principle: build practical tools, keep them simple to use, and make them work for the community they are made for.
